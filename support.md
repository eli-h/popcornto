# PopcornTO Support

PopcornTO shows every showing at Toronto's independent cinemas in one place: the Fox Theatre, the Paradise Theatre, the Revue Cinema, and Imagine Cinemas' Carlton and Market Square.

Something not working, a showtime that looks wrong, or a cinema you'd like added? Email **[popcornto@icloud.com](mailto:popcornto@icloud.com)**.

## Common questions

**A showing is missing, or a time looks wrong.**
PopcornTO reads each cinema's own website, usually first thing each morning. Pull down on the list to load the latest. If the cinema's site has changed, it may take a little while for PopcornTO to catch up; please let us know.

**How do I buy tickets?**
Tap a showing, then **Buy Tickets**. You'll buy them on the cinema's own website.

**How do I switch cinemas?**
Tap the cinema's name at the top of the list to choose another, or **All Theatres** to see them together.

**What are film alerts?**
Tell PopcornTO what you're waiting for (a film, a director, an actor, a genre, or a decade) and it lets you know when a matching showing is listed.

**I'm not getting reminders or alerts.**
Check that notifications are on for PopcornTO in **Settings › Notifications**, and that Background App Refresh is on in **Settings › General › Background App Refresh**.

**Where does the cast and crew come from?**
From [The Movie Database (TMDB)](https://www.themoviedb.org). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

**Is PopcornTO made by the cinemas?**
No. PopcornTO is an independent app and isn't affiliated with any of the cinemas it lists.
