# PopcornTO Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

PopcornTO shows showtimes for Toronto's independent cinemas. It's built to work without knowing anything about you.

## What PopcornTO collects

Nothing. PopcornTO has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We don't collect, store, or share any personal information, and we have no servers that receive data from the app.

## What stays on your iPhone

These are saved only on your device, and are removed if you delete the app:

- The theatre you chose, your filters, and your sort order
- Reminders you set for showings (delivered as notifications by your iPhone)
- Film alerts you create, and which films they've found
- Saved showtimes, posters, and cast and crew details, so the app works quickly and offline
- The showtimes shown in PopcornTO's widgets

## Who the app talks to

To show you what's playing, PopcornTO loads information from these services. Like any website you visit, they receive your device's internet address and the request, and their own privacy policies apply:

- **The cinemas' websites** (the Fox Theatre, the Paradise Theatre, the Revue Cinema, and Imagine Cinemas), for showtimes, film details, and posters. When you buy tickets, you do so on the cinema's own site, not in PopcornTO.
- **The Movie Database (TMDB)**, for cast and crew, and for the film and people search when creating an alert. The film titles and names being looked up are sent to TMDB. [TMDB's privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy)
- **YouTube**, only when you play a trailer. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Apple Maps**, for the map on a theatre's page and for directions.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: only for the reminders and film alerts you set up. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.
- **Calendar**: when you add a showing to your calendar, iOS shows its own event screen and you choose whether to save it. PopcornTO can't read your calendar.
- **Background App Refresh**: lets PopcornTO check for new showtimes early each morning, so your widgets and alerts are up to date.

PopcornTO doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, or camera.

## Children

PopcornTO doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
